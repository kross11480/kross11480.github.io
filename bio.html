His experience spans software stack components, the system design of electronic control units, and leading product development teams shipping components in electric vehicles. Long bio

158 words

Hritam Dutta is a system data architect at Robert Bosch in Stuttgart, where he works on the data systems behind autonomous driving: incident trigger-based event recording and shadow mode for L4+ vehicles, and automated root cause analysis of the drive data behind disengagements. Previously, as a platform architect at Bosch, he designed consensus mechanisms between redundant ECUs for fault tolerant autonomous driving, and specified an autonomous driving ECU together with a vehicle manufacturer.

Before Bosch he spent six years at SMR Automotive as a system architect and technical project lead, delivering the first hybrid digital mirror system in an OEM electric vehicle platform. His research career began at the University of Erlangen-Nuremberg, where he earned a Dr.-Ing. in computer science for work on the synthesis and exploration of loop accelerators for systems-on-chip, and published 23 peer-reviewed papers on compilers for FPGAs, DSPs and GPUs. He teaches automotive engineering at a university.
