Hello

I am Professor of Embedded Systems Design at TH Nürnberg, where my research area is intelligent embedded systems.

I came to teaching from twenty years in industry. Most recently, as a system data architect at Robert Bosch, I worked on the systems that let cars drive themselves: software stack components, the design of electronic control units, and leading product development teams shipping components in electric vehicles.

Before industry, I spent six years in academic research on compilers for highly parallel embedded processors, which is still how I think about most problems: find the structure, then exploit it. I also write about machine learning and systems engineering when a topic will not leave me alone.

What I work on

  • Autonomous driving data systems: incident trigger-based event recording and shadow mode for L4+ autonomous vehicles.
  • Root cause analysis: automated offline analysis of the drive data behind disengagements of a self-driving car.
  • Fault tolerant architecture: consensus between redundant ECUs to keep an autonomous driving system available when something fails.
  • Intelligent embedded systems: my research area at TH Nürnberg.
  • Teaching: Embedded Systems Design, building on earlier courses in embedded systems and hardware/software co-design.
